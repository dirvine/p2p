# P2P Foundation License Enforcement Policy

## Overview

This document outlines how MaidSafe Limited enforces licensing for the P2P Foundation project. Our goal is to ensure sustainable development while being fair and reasonable with our community.

## Enforcement Philosophy

We believe in:
- **Education before enforcement** - Help users understand licensing
- **Good faith engagement** - Assume positive intent
- **Reasonable timelines** - Allow time for compliance
- **Proportional response** - Match enforcement to violation severity
- **Community support** - Maintain positive relationships

## License Monitoring

### Automated Detection

We use various methods to identify potential license violations:

1. **Public Repository Scanning**
   - GitHub/GitLab/Bitbucket search
   - Package registry analysis
   - Dependency graph tracking

2. **Network Analysis**
   - Public service discovery
   - Technology stack detection
   - Commercial usage indicators

3. **Community Reports**
   - User notifications
   - Competitor alerts
   - Partner feedback

### Manual Review

All potential violations undergo manual review:
- Technical analysis of usage
- Business model evaluation
- Revenue assessment
- Good faith determination

## Violation Categories

### Level 1: Unintentional Violations
**Examples**:
- Misunderstanding license terms
- Accidental commercial use
- Missing attribution

**Response**:
- Educational email
- 30-day correction period
- Free consultation call
- Documentation assistance

### Level 2: Minor Violations
**Examples**:
- Small business exceeding revenue threshold
- Internal use at commercial entity
- Incomplete AGPL compliance

**Response**:
- Formal notice
- 60-day compliance window
- License migration assistance
- Discounted commercial terms

### Level 3: Significant Violations
**Examples**:
- Clear commercial use without license
- Removing license notices
- Circumventing restrictions

**Response**:
- Cease and desist letter
- 30-day response requirement
- Standard commercial rates
- Potential back-licensing fees

### Level 4: Egregious Violations
**Examples**:
- Willful infringement
- Previous violation history
- Bad faith actions
- Competitive misuse

**Response**:
- Immediate legal action
- Injunctive relief
- Damages claim
- Public enforcement

## Enforcement Process

### 1. Discovery Phase (Days 1-7)
```
Potential Violation Identified
           ↓
   Technical Analysis
           ↓
   Business Analysis
           ↓
   Initial Classification
```

### 2. Verification Phase (Days 8-14)
```
Evidence Collection
           ↓
    Legal Review
           ↓
 Violation Confirmed?
    ↙           ↘
  No              Yes
   |               |
Close          Continue
```

### 3. Engagement Phase (Days 15+)

**First Contact** (Day 15):
```
Subject: P2P Foundation Licensing Inquiry

Dear [Name],

We've noticed your use of P2P Foundation at [Project/Company].
We'd like to ensure you have the appropriate license for your use case.

This is not a legal notice - we're reaching out to help you understand
your options and find a solution that works for everyone.

Please respond within 14 days so we can assist you.

Best regards,
P2P Foundation Licensing Team
```

**Follow-up** (Day 30):
- Second email if no response
- Attempt phone contact
- Check for updated contact info

**Formal Notice** (Day 45):
- Legal letterhead
- Specific violations cited
- Clear compliance requirements
- Final timeline provided

### 4. Resolution Phase

**Compliance Options**:
1. **Purchase Commercial License**
   - Standard rates apply
   - Back-licensing negotiable
   - Payment plans available

2. **Open Source Project**
   - Full AGPL compliance
   - Source code release
   - Remove commercial features

3. **Cease Usage**
   - Removal confirmation
   - No further action
   - Future use welcomed
